# ADR NNN — Short decision title

## Status

Proposed on YYYY-MM-DD.

Usual states: Proposed, Accepted, Implemented, Superseded by ADR NNN, or
Rejected.

## Context

Describe the problem, constraints, and why an explicit decision is necessary.
Record facts and real alternatives without turning the ADR into a complete
feature specification.

## Decision

State the choice in a verifiable manner. Include limits, invariants, and what
is deliberately out of scope.

## Compatibility and data

Describe impact on schema, migrations, `.chronoproject`, backups, directories,
updater, existing installations, and offline operation. Write **No impact** only
when there truly is none.

## Consequences

- expected benefits;
- accepted costs and risks;
- future work or constraints created by the decision.

## Alternatives considered

- **Alternative:** objective reason not to adopt it.

## Validation

List tests, manual audits, metrics, or evidence required to mark the decision
as implemented.

## References

- official documentation, Issue, Pull Request, or related document.
