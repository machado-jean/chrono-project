# Code of conduct

## Commitment

We want Chrono Project to be a respectful, safe, and welcoming technical
environment regardless of experience, identity, background, disability,
appearance, belief, or form of participation.

## Expected behaviour

- discuss ideas and code; do not attack people;
- offer specific, useful, and proportionate criticism;
- accept corrections and acknowledge the limits of one’s knowledge;
- respect privacy, authorship, and confidential information;
- avoid discriminatory language, intimidation, harassment, or public exposure;
- keep Issues and Pull Requests related to the project’s purpose.

## Enforcement

Inappropriate content may be edited, hidden, or removed. Participants may
receive guidance, a warning, or a block according to severity and recurrence.
Moderation decisions must prioritise safety, context, and continuity of the
technical work.

To report a situation privately, contact the maintainer through the address
available on the public GitHub profile. Software vulnerabilities must use the
process described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
